# On AI and Authorship

A significant portion of the recent code in this project was written with the assistance of AI tools. That is worth being transparent about. It is also worth being precise about what that means.

The conversion algorithm — the deliberate rules for how an Azgaar burg becomes a CK3 barony, how culture lineages flow, how tiny kingdoms consolidate, how population drives county balance — is not AI's work. Those decisions came from a person who spent hours reading Azgaar's data model and CK3's modding documentation, and months thinking carefully about how to faithfully translate one world-building system into another. This converter started as a dream: a way to take a world you spent time crafting in Azgaar and actually play it in CK3, with the depth and political texture that game deserves. That vision is human. It belongs to someone with a genuine love for CK3 and a deep appreciation for what Azgaar makes possible as a world-building tool.

The developer behind this project has over ten years of programming experience. AI-generated code has been reviewed, challenged, and modified to the best of that experience — as part of a deliberate development process aimed at maintaining the exactness of the desired output. AI accelerated the work. It did not direct it.

If you are using this tool, you are using something that could not have existed in its current form without AI assistance. You are also using something that would not exist at all without the human intent, domain knowledge, and stubborn attention to correctness that shaped every conversion decision in it.
